# 017 — Rational logarithms

**Research draft** · [PDF](log-rational-irrationality-proof.pdf)

The manuscript derives $`\mu(\log a)=2`$ for every fixed $`a\in\mathbb Q_{>0}\setminus\{1\}`$. It extends the interpolation and determinant method used for $`\mu(\pi)=2`$ in [OpenAI 017](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/The-irrationality-exponent-of-pi-is-2-September-24-2026/paper.pdf) to rational logarithms.

**Depends on:** [OpenAI 017, Lemma 2.2](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/The-irrationality-exponent-of-pi-is-2-September-24-2026/paper.pdf#page=5), the weighted transverse multiplicity estimate, including its uniformity in the equations, the irreducible component, and the analytic frame, with no lower threshold for the degree parameter. The present manuscript proves the interpolation extension to distinct nonzero multiplicative coordinates, the rational-denominator estimates, and the determinant comparison from that input.

For every $`\nu>2`$, the resulting bound $`|\log a-p/q|\ge q^{-\nu}`$ holds for all sufficiently large denominators. The threshold depends on $`a`$ and $`\nu`$ and is ineffective; no uniform positive constant in a $`q^{-2}`$ bound is asserted.
